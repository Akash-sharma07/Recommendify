ABOUT RECOMMENDIFY

Built to Make Finding Something Good Easier.

Recommendify is a project I built to bring my book and movie recommendation systems together in one simple application.

Back to Home

THE IDEA

Why I Built Recommendify

I enjoy working on projects where machine learning can be used to solve a practical problem. Recommendation systems were one of those projects that helped me understand how data and machine learning can be turned into something people can actually use.

I had already worked on separate book and movie recommendation projects, so I decided to bring them together and build a single web application around them.

That became Recommendify.

The goal was not to make a complicated application. I wanted to keep the interface simple while letting the recommendation models do the interesting part in the background.
